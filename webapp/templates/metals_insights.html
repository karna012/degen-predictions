📚 Investment Guide

Precious Metals

  • Hedge against inflation
  • Low correlation with stocks
  • Physical & ETF options
  • Long-term holding recommended
  • Tax implications vary by region

Industrial Metals

  • Follow economic cycles
  • Supply/demand driven
  • Futures & commodity ETFs
  • Higher volatility
  • Geopolitical factors matter
