About Degen Predictions

What this site does

Degen Predictions generates free forecasts across sports, crypto, and stocks. On the sports side, that's pre-match predictions for {{ league_label }} and four other major European soccer leagues, plus MLB and NPB baseball -- match result, total goals/runs, and related markets, using statistical models trained on years of historical match data. On the crypto and stocks side, it's a ranked list of assets by predicted 6-month forward return, from a separate machine-learning model trained on price/volume history and fundamentals. All of it is built as an educational project for people curious about how data-driven forecasting works, not as a betting service or an investment advisory service.

How fixtures stay current

Upcoming fixtures and predictions refresh automatically every day, and a new round unlocks as soon as every fixture in the previous round has kicked off -- so you're always looking at the next set of real, upcoming matches rather than a static list.

Where the data comes from

Soccer: historical results and match statistics come from public football data archives, player-level stats come from Understat, and upcoming fixture schedules come from fixturedownload.com.

Baseball (MLB): historical game logs from Retrosheet, live fixtures and probable-pitcher stats from ESPN's public scoreboard API.

Baseball (NPB): schedules and results from spaia.jp's public schedule API.

Crypto: price/volume history and market data from CoinGecko's free public API; Bitcoin on-chain metrics (active addresses, hash rate) from blockchain.com. The asset universe is the top ~60 coins by market cap.

Stocks: price history and fundamentals (P/E, margins, growth, debt ratios) from Yahoo Finance via yfinance. The universe is the S&P 500 (US) and NIFTY 50 (India) constituents, sourced from their official index pages.

News sentiment (crypto, stocks, and MLB/NPB team form): recent headlines from Google News, scored locally with a lexicon-based sentiment model (VADER) -- no third-party sentiment API.

Nothing on this site uses bookmaker odds, brokerage order flow, or any paid data feed as an input -- every source above is free and publicly accessible.

Honest limitations

Sports, crypto, and stocks are all high-variance by nature -- even a well-built model will miss regularly, and a confident-looking probability or ranking is not a guarantee. Treat every prediction here as an estimate to inform your own thinking, not as a certainty. For sports, see the Past Seasons page for how these predictions have actually performed against real, completed matches. For crypto and stocks, the rankings pages are upfront about measured overfitting found in backtesting -- read that before trusting any single number.
