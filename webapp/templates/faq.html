Frequently Asked Questions

Sports predictions, Today's Picks, multi bets, MLB, tennis, EPL, La Liga, CPL — plus crypto and stocks.

General

What is Degen Predictions?

Degen Predictions is a free ML platform that forecasts sports matches, cryptocurrency rankings, and US/India stock rankings. Sometimes typed as “Degen Prediction”. Educational research — not betting or investment advice.

Is Degen Predictions free to use?

Yes. Every calculator and prediction tool on the site is completely free, with no account, login, or payment required for any category.

How accurate are the predictions?

Sports boards publish chronological out-of-sample hit rates from holdout data — not marketing numbers. Live chips and the accuracy hub use the same calibrated threshold files as Today's Picks. Crypto and stocks use walk-forward-validated rankings; those are estimates, not guarantees. Also see the MLB track record and cricket/tennis results pages.

Is Degen Predictions betting advice?

No. Forecasts are educational machine-learning estimates. The site does not promote or facilitate sports betting, gambling, or trading.

What is Degen Predictions accuracy?

Degen Predictions reports chronological out-of-sample (OOS) hit rates with sample size and coverage — full-slate baselines plus selective ~70% tiers when a market can clear that bar honestly. Start at the accuracy hub, then open league results (tennis, IPL, CPL, MLB) for predicted-vs-actual ledgers. Numbers are research estimates, not guarantees.

What categories does Degen Predictions cover?

Sports (football, baseball, cricket including IPL/CPL, and men’s ATP tennis), Crypto (ML rankings / Beat BTC by horizon), and Stocks (US S&P and India Nifty, multi-horizon predicted-return boards).

Do the Crypto and Stocks pages show live prices?

Yes. Each rankings page embeds a live TradingView candlestick chart above the table. Clicking any row switches the chart to that asset in real time, with interval controls from 1 minute up to monthly. Where a TradingView listing does not exist (mainly tokenized institutional fund products in crypto), the page shows a clear notice instead of guessing.

How should I read crypto and stock rankings?

They are walk-forward ML leans, not price targets. Absolute return estimates can be withheld when direction fails holdout checks (common on crypto — you may see Beat BTC / peer lean / ranges instead). Boards can be stale if the ranked CSV is old. Nothing here is investment advice; you can lose money.

What do crypto and stock models not claim?

They do not predict next-day candles, do not use your brokerage account, and do not guarantee beating Bitcoin or the S&P. Prefer the on-page confidence / OOS notes and the terms over any social screenshot.

Crypto

How are crypto predictions calculated?

A walk-forward-validated ML model ranks cryptocurrencies by investment window. When absolute point forecasts are withheld, the board leads with Beat BTC probability, peer lean, and an 80% return range — matching what you see on /crypto. Stablecoins are excluded. Rankings refresh regularly.

Which cryptocurrencies are covered?

About 60 digital assets, from major coins (Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana, XRP) to DeFi tokens. Live TradingView charts are available for most; a few tokenized institutional products have no exchange listing and are labelled clearly.

Is crypto riskier than the other categories on this site?

The model itself flags this: crypto predictions are especially noisy, since only about 365 days of price history are available per coin for training, compared to years of data for stocks.

Stocks

How are US and India stock predictions calculated?

For each investment window we pick the best walk-forward model (Ridge / XGBoost / LSTM / GRU). Boards on US stock predictions (S&P) and India stock predictions (Nifty) lead with predicted return; outperform % is the chance of beating the market benchmark — not a restatement of the absolute forecast.

What horizons are available?

Stocks go out to multi-year windows (up to 25 years where history allows). Crypto public boards stop earlier because longer crypto history is too thin for a credible forecast.
