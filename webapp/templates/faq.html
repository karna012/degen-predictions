Frequently Asked Questions

Everything in one place -- general platform questions plus specifics for every category, covering both physical assets (Dubai real estate, metals) and digital assets (crypto, stocks).

General

What is Degen Predictions?

A free platform that uses machine learning (ML) and AI to forecast outcomes across five categories: football, baseball, and cricket match predictions, cryptocurrency rankings, stock rankings, Dubai real estate ROI, and metals price forecasts.

Is Degen Predictions free to use?

Yes. Every calculator and prediction tool on the site is completely free, with no account, login, or payment required for any category.

How accurate are the predictions?

Each category is powered by models trained on real historical data -- walk-forward-validated ranking models for crypto/stocks, historically-grounded appreciation/volatility models for Dubai real estate and metals, and ELO ratings and ensemble ML for sports. All forecasts are estimates, not guarantees, and are clearly labelled as such.

What categories does Degen Predictions cover?

Crypto (AI-ranked digital assets by predicted 6-month return), Stocks (US and India rankings by predicted return), Dubai Real Estate (a physical-asset property ROI calculator), Metals (physical precious and industrial metal price forecasts with live spot pricing), and Sports (football, baseball, and cricket match predictions).

Do the Crypto, Stocks, and Metals pages show live prices?

Yes. Each of those three pages embeds a live TradingView candlestick chart above the calculator/rankings table. Clicking any row (or, on Metals, changing the dropdown) switches the chart to that asset in real time, with interval controls from 1 minute up to monthly. Not every asset has a matching TradingView listing -- where one doesn't exist (mainly tokenized institutional fund products in crypto), the page shows a clear "not found in TradingView" notice instead of a chart, rather than guessing.

Crypto (Digital Assets)

How are the crypto rankings calculated?

A walk-forward-validated ML model ranks cryptocurrencies by predicted 6-month forward return, using price momentum, volatility, market capitalization, and news sentiment as inputs. Rankings refresh daily.

Which cryptocurrencies are covered?

60 digital assets, from major coins (Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana, XRP) to DeFi tokens and stablecoins. Live TradingView charts are available for 50 of the 60 -- the other 10 are tokenized institutional fund products (e.g. BlackRock's BUIDL, Hashnote's USYC) with no real exchange listing anywhere, not a gap in this site's data.

Is crypto riskier than the other categories on this site?

The model itself flags this: crypto predictions are noted as especially noisy, since only 365 days of price history are available per coin for training, compared to years of data for stocks and real estate.

Stocks (Digital Assets)

How are stock rankings calculated?

The same 6-month forward-return ranking approach as crypto, but using stock-specific fundamentals (like P/E ratio) and price trend data. US and India stocks are modeled and ranked separately.

How many stocks are covered, and is live chart data available?

553 stocks total (roughly 500 US, 50 India). Every single one has a verified, working live TradingView chart -- 100% coverage, confirmed directly against TradingView's own data rather than assumed.

Are US and India stock predictions equally reliable?

No -- India stock predictions have shown weaker out-of-sample signal than US stocks in backtesting. Always check the data_confidence column on the rankings table before acting on either.

Dubai Real Estate (Physical Asset)

How is Dubai real estate ROI calculated?

ROI is the sum of capital appreciation (compound growth on the property value, adjusted by an area-specific infrastructure multiplier) plus rental income over your chosen holding period, shown as a percentage of your original investment.

What is a good ROI for Dubai real estate?

Dubai properties typically target a combined 6-10% annual return (capital appreciation plus rental yield). Areas with major infrastructure development -- new metro lines, roads, or master-planned communities -- can significantly exceed this range in the model's forecasts, with total combined ROI reaching 8-16%+ annually in areas like Creek Harbour or Al Barsha.

Does the calculator account for new metro stations and infrastructure?

Yes. Each of the 7 modeled areas has an infrastructure development multiplier based on metro proximity, development phase (emerging/growth/mature), and commercial density, which adjusts the base appreciation rate up or down accordingly.

How long should I hold a Dubai property for the best returns?

The calculator supports holding periods from 6 months to 35 years. Infrastructure-driven growth tends to be strongest in the first 10 years of a development phase, after which the model gradually tapers the multiplier as an area matures.

Does the calculator include buying costs like the DLD transfer fee?

No -- the calculator's ROI figures are gross capital appreciation and gross rental income only. Real transaction costs (4% DLD transfer fee, 2% agency commission, trustee/title deed fees, and annual service charges) typically total 5-9% of the purchase price and are covered in detail on the Investment Guide page, not baked into the calculator's output.

Is this Dubai ROI calculator free?

Yes, completely free with no account or login required. Degen Predictions is an educational/research platform, not a real estate brokerage or financial advisory service.

Metals (Physical Asset)

How accurate are metal price predictions?

Forecasts are based on each metal's historical annual appreciation rate and volatility, adjusted by a market-cycle multiplier. They are estimates, not guarantees -- commodity prices are influenced by supply/demand shifts, geopolitical events, and macroeconomic conditions no model can fully anticipate.

Are the metal prices actually live, or fixed estimates?

Both, depending on the metal. Gold, Silver, Platinum, Palladium, Copper, Steel, and Aluminum refresh daily from real market data feeds. Zinc, Nickel, and Lithium have no free live data source anywhere (verified directly, not assumed) and stay as periodically-updated benchmarks -- this is clearly labelled on the Market Insights page.

What is the average annual appreciation rate for gold?

This model uses a base annual appreciation rate of roughly 8.5% for gold, before applying its market-cycle multiplier based on historical volatility. Silver, lithium, and other higher-volatility metals use higher base rates to reflect their historically larger price swings.

What happened to gemstones -- didn't this used to cover diamonds and rubies?

Gemstones were removed. Unlike metals, gemstones have no single quoted market price -- a "1-carat diamond" can vary 100x in price depending on cut, color, and clarity grading. Rather than show an invented number with no real source behind it, the category now covers only precious and industrial metals, which do have real, verifiable spot prices.

Is the metals calculator free to use?

Yes, completely free with no account or login required. Degen Predictions is an educational/research platform, not a commodities broker or investment advisory service.
